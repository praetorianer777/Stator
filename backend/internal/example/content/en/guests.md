# Guests

%%properties%%

| Audience | Administrators |
|---|---|
| Reading time | 2 minutes |

%%end%%

A guest is somebody from outside the organization let into one space: a customer, a partner, a contractor.

## Inviting a guest

**Guests** in **Space settings**, for administrators of the organization, and **Invite a guest**. Give the email address they sign in with at your identity provider and what they **May** do: **Read**, **Read and comment**, or **Read, comment and edit**. They sign in as anybody does; no link is mailed.

## What a guest sees

- Only that space and the people in it: never other spaces, personal spaces or the organization's list of people.
- Only what they were granted, even where a space lets everyone in the organization do more.
- No access tokens and nothing of the organization as a whole.

**Remove** takes a guest out again. Personal spaces take no guests, and every invitation is recorded in the audit log.
