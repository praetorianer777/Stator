# Watching and notifications

%%properties%%

| Audience | Everybody |
|---|---|
| Reading time | 3 minutes |

%%end%%

## Watching

**Watch** in a page's header watches the page, the page and every page below it, or the whole space, and shows who is watching. You watch the pages you create and publish without asking, unless you turn that off in your notification settings. **Watch blog** in a space's blog watches its new posts.

Watchers hear of a version published with **Notify the people watching this page** ticked, and of new pages published below what they watch. **Watching** in the account menu lists all you watch, with **Stop watching**.

## Notifications

The bell in the top bar counts what you have not read: mentions, tasks given to you and their due days, replies, comments on watched pages, new pages and posts, pages shared with you and more. **Mark all as read** clears it.

**Notifications** in the account menu chooses, for each kind, whether it reaches you in the app, by email or both, and whether emails go out one by one, once an hour or once a day. A bundle leaves out what you read in the app already.

## Sharing a page

**Share** in a page's header sends it to people and groups with a note. It gives nobody access: only people who may already see the page are told, and the dialog says who may.

**Export as PDF** in the **Page actions** hands a page on as a file: the published page as you read it, with every block drawn and the page's title, version and page numbers on every sheet. A public page and a public link offer **PDF** beside the title.
