# Moving pages in and out

%%properties%%

| Audience | Everybody |
|---|---|
| Reading time | 2 minutes |

%%end%%

## A page as a file

- **Export as PDF** in the **Page actions** prints the published page with its title, version and page numbers on every sheet.
- **Export as Word** beside it hands the page on as a document to edit offline.
- A public page and a public link offer both beside the title.

## A whole space

Administrators of a space find **Export** in **Space settings**. The export is made in the background and kept for a day to download.

- **Archive** holds everything in the space, every version and file, comments and permissions, to import again here or in another organization.
- **HTML pages** are the published pages as web pages, to read offline in any browser.

**Import space** on the spaces overview makes a new space from an archive, or from the space export of another wiki as a zip. People are found by their email address, and the import lists whom it did not find.

## Documents into pages

- **Import Word documents** in the **Page actions** makes pages below a page from .docx files, several at once or a .zip with folders.
- **Import Markdown** does the same from .md files, and links between the files lead to the pages they became.

> [!NOTE]
> Drafts stay where they are: an export holds what readers read.
