# Comments and reactions

%%properties%%

| Audience | Everybody |
|---|---|
| Reading time | 2 minutes |

%%end%%

Everybody in the organization may comment in this space, so try what this page explains right here: the comment below is waiting for a reply.

## Comments on a page

At the foot of a published page, **Add a comment** and **Comment**, or Mod+Enter. A thread takes replies, and each comment can be edited or deleted by whoever wrote it. Commenting takes the space permission **Add comments**; an administrator of the space may delete anybody's comment, which the audit log records.

## Comments on a passage

Select some words of a published page and click **Comment** beside them, or press Ctrl+Alt+M. The thread sits beside its passage, and **Resolve** puts it away once it is settled; **Reopen** brings it back. A thread whose words were changed or taken out stays listed under **Passages no longer on the page**.

## Mentions

Typing @ and a name in a comment, or in a page, tells that person. Stator marks the people who could not see the page, so you know they will not be told.

## Reactions

**Add a reaction** below a page or a comment adds an emoji: a few are offered at once and **More emoji** finds any by its English name. Clicking a reaction adds yours or takes it back. Whoever may comment may react.
