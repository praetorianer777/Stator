# Armature

%%properties%%

| Audience | Everybody |
|---|---|
| Reading time | 3 minutes |

%%end%%

Stator works hand in hand with Armature, where teams plan and track their work as issues. Both sign people in through the same identity provider, so a person is the same person in both.

## Connecting

1. An administrator of the organization connects it under **Armature** in the account menu, with Armature's address and the organization there.
2. Each person makes a token in Armature, not marked read only, and pastes it into **Armature token** in their profile.

Every question to Armature is asked as the person reading, so a page never shows an issue its reader could not open in Armature.

## Issues in pages

- Typing an issue key such as CP-12 and a space or a punctuation mark turns it into a chip with the issue's status; so does pasting an issue's address.
- The slash menu offers an **Armature issue** card, an **Armature issue list** from a query, an **Armature chart** and an **Armature roadmap**; a **Calendar** shows a project's due issues too.
- Select some text or list items in the editor and **Create Armature issue** files them as issues, one by one, and puts their chips in the text.

{{if .Armature}}Each of them is on [Every block a page can hold](showcase.md), from the project {{.Armature.Project}}.{{else}}This organization has no Armature that showed whoever made this space a project, so [Every block a page can hold](showcase.md) says so in place of the Armature blocks.{{end}}

## Back in Armature

When a page that names an issue is published, the issue lists the page in Armature. The page shows under **Linked in Armature** whether each link is made. Following your Armature theme is a choice under **Themes** in the account menu.
