# The page tree and folders

%%properties%%

| Audience | Editors |
|---|---|
| Reading time | 3 minutes |

%%end%%

The sidebar of a space shows its pages as a tree. The arrow keys walk it, Enter opens a page, and M moves the page in focus.

## Moving, copying and ordering

- Drag a page in the tree: dropped on the top or bottom edge of another page it goes before or after it, dropped on its middle it goes below it.
- **Move** in the page's **Page actions** menu (the three dots) puts it under another page, in this space or another, with the pages below it or without them.
- **Copy** makes a copy under a new title, with the pages below it if you ask.
- The home page stays where it is: it is the root of the space.

## Folders

A **folder** holds pages and other folders and has no text of its own, so a tree can be grouped without writing a page to hold it. **New folder** in **Page actions** makes one. This guide groups its pages in folders such as **Writing** and **Access**, and the folder below this page collects the meeting notes the showcase's template button makes.

## The trash and the archive

- **Move to trash** in **Page actions** takes the page and every page below it out of the tree. The space's **Trash** in the sidebar puts them back with **Restore**, where they were, or under the home page when that place is gone. Only administrators of the space delete for good.
- **Archive** keeps a page and the pages below it readable at their addresses while taking them out of the tree, search and Home. Administrators of the space take them out of the archive again.

## The hub

Administrators of the organization may choose one page as its **Hub**, under **Hub page** in the account menu. Everybody finds it in the navigation, and may land on it whenever they open Stator.
