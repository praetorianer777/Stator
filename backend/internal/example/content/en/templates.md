# Templates

| Audience | Editors |
|---|---|

%%end%%

## Page templates

**New page** offers **Start from**: a blank page, or one of the templates that come with Stator, such as **Meeting notes**, **How-to guide**, **Troubleshooting article**, **Retrospective**, **Decision record**, **Product requirements** and **Project plan**. Each shows a preview before **Create page**.

- A template's grey hints say what goes where and disappear as you type; any left over are taken out when the page is published.
- A date in a template's title becomes the day the page is made.

## Your own templates

Administrators of the organization keep templates every space offers under **Templates** in the account menu, and administrators of a space keep its own under **Templates** in the space's settings. A template of your own can ask for variables, such as a customer, a day, a choice or a person: **Add variable**, then its **Insert** button puts its blank where the caret is. Whoever makes a page, a blog post or a template button's page from it fills them in under **Fill in the template**, and Stator puts the values into the new page.

## Template buttons

A **Template button** on a page makes a new page from a template in one click, under the page you chose or at the top of a space, titled as you said, and opens it for writing. Use it for what a team makes again and again: [Every block a page can hold](showcase.md) has one that adds meeting notes to the **Meeting notes** folder.

## Space templates

A new space can start from a space template, with its first pages, labels and permissions: **Knowledge base**, **Team space** or **Documentation**. See [Spaces and pages](spaces-and-pages.md).
