# Drafts, publishing and history

%%properties%%

| Audience | Editors |
|---|---|
| Reading time | 3 minutes |

%%end%%

## Drafts

**Edit** opens the editor. What you write is saved as your draft about a second after you stop typing, and nobody else sees it until you publish. Leave and come back: the page says you have a draft and offers **Continue editing**. **Discard draft** throws it away.

A page you just made is unpublished: only you see it until its first publish.

## Publishing

**Publish**, or Mod+Enter, opens the publish dialog:

1. **What changed** is a note for the history, if you like.
2. **Notify the people watching this page** tells its watchers; leave it ticked for a change they should hear about.
3. **When** is **Now**, or **At a set time**, which [Scheduled publishing](scheduled-publishing.md) explains.

When somebody published while you were editing, Stator does not publish over them: it offers to compare your draft with their version, or to keep your draft and publish it anyway.

## History

**History** in the page's header lists every published version, newest first, with who published it, when and what changed.

- **View** shows a version as it was.
- **Compare with previous**, or two versions ticked and **Compare selected**, shows what changed: added words underlined, removed ones struck through.
- **Restore** publishes an old version again as the newest one. Nothing is ever lost from the history.
