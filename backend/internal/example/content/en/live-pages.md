# Live pages

%%properties%%

| Audience | Editors |
|---|---|
| Reading time | 2 minutes |

%%end%%

Some pages are never finished: working notes, a running agenda, a list kept by everybody. A **live** page has no drafts: what its editors type becomes the page about a second later, and its readers follow along.

## Making a page live

**Editing mode** in **Page actions** offers **Drafts and publishing** or **Live**. Whoever may edit the page may switch it; the switch is recorded in the organization's audit log. Folders and blog posts are never live.

## What changes

- The page carries a **Live** badge, and the editor's button is **Done** rather than **Publish**. Mod+Enter saves at once.
- The history keeps one version for each ten minutes of work, naming everybody who typed into it, so it still reads in steps.
- Everything else stays: comments, labels, restrictions and watching work as on any page.
