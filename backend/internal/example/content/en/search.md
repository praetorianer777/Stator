# Search

%%properties%%

| Audience | Everybody |
|---|---|
| Reading time | 2 minutes |

%%end%%

## Quick search

Ctrl+K, or Cmd+K on a Mac, or the search box in the top bar opens the quick search. It finds pages by the start of their titles and lists the pages you opened last; Enter runs a full search.

## Full search

**Search** in the navigation reads titles, the words of pages, comments and the names of files. Write words, "a phrase" in quotes, or -word to leave a word out. Capitals and accents do not matter.

The filters narrow it to a space, to pages, files or comments, to labels, to who changed something and when; archived pages are left out unless you ask for them. Results come by best match, or newest change first.

You find only what you may read: a restricted page never shows up for somebody it is closed to.
