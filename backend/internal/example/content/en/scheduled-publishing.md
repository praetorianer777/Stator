# Scheduled publishing

%%properties%%

| Audience | Editors |
|---|---|
| Reading time | 2 minutes |

%%end%%

A change can go out later: an announcement on Monday morning, a policy on the day it takes effect.

## Scheduling

In the publish dialog choose **At a set time**, pick the moment in **Publish at**, in your own time zone, and **Schedule**. The time has to be ahead, and at most a year ahead.

Your draft goes out as it stands at that moment, so keep editing until then. The page tells you when it goes out, with **Change** and **Cancel schedule**, and tells its other editors whose publish is planned. One publish is planned for a page at a time.

## When it cannot go out

If the page is archived or deleted by then, you lost the right to edit it, or somebody published in between, nothing is published. You are told, and **Edit and schedule again** takes you back to your draft.

> [!TIP]
> Blog posts can be scheduled too; a post is then dated by the moment it goes out.
