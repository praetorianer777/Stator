# Anonymous spaces and public links

%%properties%%

| Audience | Administrators |
|---|---|
| Reading time | 3 minutes |

%%end%%

Some pages are for everybody, signed in or not: a handbook for customers, a status page, a public plan.

## Spaces anybody may read

Two switches open a space. An administrator of the organization turns on **Let anybody read the spaces that allow it** under **Reading without signing in** in **Permissions** in the account menu. Then an administrator of a space turns on **Let anybody read this space without signing in** in its permissions. Personal spaces are never open.

Somebody who is not signed in reads published pages only, with a search of their own. They never see names, comments, reactions, drafts or restricted pages.

## Public links

A public link opens one page to whoever holds it. The organization allows them with **Allow public links** under **Public links** in **Permissions**; turning that off stops every link at once.

- **Share** on a page, then **Make a public link**, with a label and when it runs out, if ever. Whoever may edit the page may make one.
- The link is shown once: copy it then. It opens that page alone, without the pages below, comments, history or names.
- **Revoke** in the list of live links ends it. Making and revoking links is recorded in the audit log.
- Pages of personal spaces, folders, unpublished and restricted pages get no public links.
