# Spaces and pages

%%properties%%

| Audience | Everybody |
|---|---|
| Reading time | 3 minutes |

%%end%%

%%excerpt What Stator is%%

Stator keeps a team's knowledge as pages. Pages live in **spaces**, one for each team or topic, and every space starts from its home page, with the pages below it in a tree.

%%end%%

## Spaces

- **Spaces** in the navigation lists every space you may see. **Create space** makes one, for whoever holds the permission **Create spaces**; administrators of the organization always do.
- A space has a name, a description and a **key** of two to ten capital letters or digits, such as DOCS. The key is part of every address in the space and never changes.
- A new space starts blank, with only its home page, or from a space template: **Knowledge base**, **Team space** or **Documentation**, each with its first pages, labels and permissions. Whoever makes a space administers it.
- **Create your personal space** makes a space of your own, which only you see until you share it in its permissions.
- The star beside a space or page keeps it under **Starred** on **Home**, beside what you viewed and edited lately and the updates of what you watch.

## Space settings

**Space settings** in a space's sidebar is for its administrators. **Details** holds the name and description, and the buttons to archive or delete the space. **Shortcuts** pins links to pages or web addresses above the page tree, each shown only to whoever may open its page. **Permissions**, **Guests**, **Trash** and **Archive** have guides of their own.

An archived space stays readable at every address but leaves the space list, search and Home, and nothing in it changes until an administrator takes it out of the archive again. Deleting a space deletes every page in it, for good.

## Pages

A page has a title and a body made of blocks: text, lists, tables, panels and many more, all shown in [Every block a page can hold](showcase.md). **New page** in a page's header makes a page below it, blank or from a template. A new page is yours alone until you publish it; see [Drafts, publishing and history](drafts-and-history.md).

**Import Word documents** in the **Page actions** makes pages below a page from .docx files: headings, lists, tables, pictures and links come across, and the import lists what did not. One document becomes a page at once; several, or a .zip with folders, are imported in the background, each folder a page of its own.
