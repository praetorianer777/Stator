# Editing together

%%properties%%

| Audience | Editors |
|---|---|
| Reading time | 2 minutes |

%%end%%

Everybody who opens a page's editor at the same time writes in one shared draft and sees each other's changes as they are typed.

- The avatars of the people editing show in the editor, and each of them has a caret of their own colour.
- Undo takes back your own changes only.
- Offline, keep writing: what you type is kept in the browser, even when the tab closes, and joins the others' when the connection returns.
- When nobody can be reached within a few seconds, the editor works on your own draft as usual.
- **Discard draft** throws the shared draft away for everybody editing.
- Publishing works as always: whoever publishes, publishes the draft everybody wrote.

Only people who may edit the page join. When your session ends or somebody takes away your right to edit, the editor stops sharing and tells you why.
