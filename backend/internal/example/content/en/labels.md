# Labels

%%properties%%

| Audience | Everybody |
|---|---|
| Reading time | 2 minutes |

%%end%%

A label is a word on a page that groups it with others, across the tree and across spaces. Every page of this guide carries the label **guide**, and one more for its part.

- **Add a label** below a page, for whoever may edit it. A label is lower case letters, digits, hyphens, underscores and dots, up to 40 of them.
- Clicking a label opens its page, which lists the pages carrying it in this space, or in every space.
- Search narrows its results to some labels.
