# Permissions

%%properties%%

| Audience | Administrators |
|---|---|
| Reading time | 4 minutes |

%%end%%

Who may do what is decided in three places, from the whole organization down to one page. The database holds every rule as well, so nothing gets around them.

## The organization

**Permissions** in the account menu, for administrators of the organization, grants **Use Stator** and **Create spaces** to people and groups. **Administer the organization** follows the roles of owner and administrator; administrators may do everything in every space.

## A space

**Permissions** in **Space settings** is a grid of people and groups against **View**, **Add pages**, **Add comments**, **Delete** and **Administer**. Each permission includes viewing, and administering includes all of them. A space always keeps one administrator.

In this space everybody may view and comment, so the guides stay as they are while everybody tries comments and reactions.

## Copying permissions

**Copy from another space** in a space's permissions takes the table of a space you administer, to replace this one or to merge into it. You see every change before **Copy permissions**. Page restrictions and guests are not copied, and a copy that would leave the space without an administrator is refused.

## A page

**Restrictions** in **Page actions** narrows who may view and who may edit a page and every page below it, within what the space allows. The dialog shows what pages above already restrict, and refuses a change that would lock you out. A restricted page shows a **Restricted** badge.

## Checking access

**Check access** in **Page actions**, for administrators of the space, says for a person whether they may view, edit, move to the trash and comment on a page, and which permission or restriction decides it.
