# Blog posts

| Audience | Everybody |
|---|---|

%%end%%

Every space has a **Blog** in its sidebar, for news and announcements that belong to a day rather than a place in the tree. This space's blog has two posts already.

## Writing a post

- **New post** in the blog asks for a title, then **Start writing** opens the editor. Whoever may add pages to the space may post.
- A post is a page outside the tree: it has drafts, history, comments, reactions, labels and restrictions like any page, but it is never moved, copied or made live, and no page goes below it.
- Its date is the moment it is first published, and it never moves. To choose the date, schedule the post.
- Your posts that have not gone out yet are listed for you alone.

## Reading the blog

The blog lists its posts newest first, and **Posts by date** goes to a year or a month with the number of posts in each. **Watch blog** tells you of every new post.
