refused by the migrate Job, which changes nothing and names them; split the
instance first as docs/organization-transfer.md says. When the migrate Job
fails, docs/upgrading.md says how to read why and what to do.

Reads go to {{ . }} when the replica a read reaches has caught up, otherwise to the primary.
{{- else }}
Reads go there too: no read replica is configured.
{{- end }}
